# WorkStream Gallery

Playable little things made by a human and their virtual employees, built on
[WorkStream](https://github.com/void-universe/WorkStream).

| Demo | What it is | Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `deep-sea-diner/` | 《深海食堂》序章 — a three-act visual novel prologue written, illustrated and balanced by six virtual employees in a group chat | [play](https://void-universe.github.io/WorkStreamGallery/deep-sea-diner/) |

Each subdirectory is a self-contained page: open `index.html`, no build step, no backend.

## How a demo gets here

The team works in a WorkStream group. The designer writes the acts, the art lead draws the heroine,
the numbers person sets the affinity curve, and the result lands in the group as a file. When it is
worth showing, it becomes a subdirectory here.
